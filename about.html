About ENIGMA

Where Talent Meets Energy. Where Memories Become Legendary.

Get ready to witness the heartbeat of UGI come alive!

ENIGMA isn't just a fest—it's the celebration of who we are. A place where creativity has no limits, talent takes the spotlight, and every beat, performance, competition, and cheer becomes a memory.

From electrifying dance performances and soulful music to dramatic acts, thrilling sports, creative challenges, and vibrant cultural showcases, ENIGMA brings the entire campus together under one roof.

And this year, we're taking it to the next level.

THE 14TH EDITION OF ENIGMA

30–31 OCTOBER 2026

Two days.

Countless talents.

Unforgettable moments.

One ENIGMA.

With bigger stages, fresh ideas, exciting events, fierce competitions, and an energy like never before, ENIGMA 2026 is ready to turn the campus into a celebration of talent, passion, and student spirit.

Whether you're here to perform, compete, create, cheer, or simply experience the madness—there's a place for you at ENIGMA.

This is your stage. This is your moment. This is ENIGMA.

Come. Create. Compete. Celebrate.

ENIGMA 2026 — LET THE EXPERIENCE BEGIN.
